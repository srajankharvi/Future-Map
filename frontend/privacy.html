Introduction

Welcome to Future Map. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Future Map collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our platform.

By accessing or using Future Map, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Username
  • Password (stored securely in encrypted/hashed form)

Career & Educational Information

To provide personalized recommendations and guidance, we may collect:

  • Career interests
  • Skills and strengths
  • Educational preferences
  • Assessment responses
  • Selected career paths and roadmaps

AI Mock Interview Data

When using our AI-powered interview features, we may collect:

  • Interview responses
  • Interview performance metrics
  • AI-generated feedback
  • Interview history

Usage Information

We automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Pages visited
  • Session activity
  • Date and time of access

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide personalized career recommendations
  • Generate learning and career roadmaps
  • Deliver AI mock interview experiences
  • Track user progress and activity
  • Improve platform performance and functionality
  • Analyze platform usage trends
  • Enhance security and prevent misuse
  • Develop and improve platform features

AI-Generated Content

Future Map uses artificial intelligence technologies to provide:

  • Career recommendations
  • Learning suggestions
  • Project recommendations
  • Skill development guidance
  • Mock interview questions
  • Interview feedback

AI-generated content is provided for informational and educational purposes only and should not be considered professional career, educational, legal, or employment advice.

Cookies and Session Technologies

Future Map may use cookies and session technologies to:

  • Maintain user login sessions
  • Improve security
  • Enhance user experience
  • Remember preferences
  • Monitor platform performance

Disabling cookies may affect certain platform features and functionality.

Data Security

We implement multiple security measures designed to protect user information, including:

  • Secure HTTPS connections
  • Password hashing
  • Session management controls
  • Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) protection
  • Content Security Policy (CSP)
  • Input validation and sanitization
  • Rate limiting and abuse prevention
  • Secure database practices

While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Sharing

Future Map does not sell, rent, or trade user personal information.

Information may be disclosed only when:

  • Required by applicable law
  • Necessary to protect platform security
  • Needed to prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Required to protect the rights, safety, or property of Future Map and its users

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide platform services
  • Maintain user accounts
  • Improve platform functionality
  • Meet legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce policies

Users may request account deletion where applicable.

User Rights

Depending on applicable laws, users may have the right to:

  • Access their personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of their account and associated data
  • Request information regarding stored data
  • Withdraw consent where legally applicable

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect:

  • New features and services
  • Security improvements
  • Legal and regulatory requirements
  • Operational changes

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

Future Map is committed to protecting user privacy while providing secure, personalized, and innovative career guidance and learning experiences.
